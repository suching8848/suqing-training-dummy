[h1]蘇清的训练假人[/h1]
在基地建造一个训练假人，用平时的攻击操作练习斧头、长棍、短棍、长刀、短刀和长矛。有效命中后，经验按武器的原版分类写入相应技能。

[list]
[*]在原版建造菜单的「蘇清的训练假人」分类建造。需要木工 1 和锤子，消耗 4 块木板、6 枚钉子、4 份碎布。
[*]右键查看耐久。假人初始有 300 点耐久，每次有效近战命中固定消耗 1 点。可用锤子、1 块木板和 1 份碎布恢复最多 100 点耐久。
[*]近战命中默认给 0.5 基础 XP，沙盒范围 0–100。原版经验倍率照常计算。
[*]试验性枪械训练默认关闭。开启后，同层、至少 3 格距离且无遮挡的射击可按瞄线判定训练命中，默认给 1 基础瞄准 XP。枪械模组和 Viewpoint 下的瞄准行为待验收。
[/list]

[b]Build 42.22.2 单人[/b]。主模组为 [b]SuQingTrainingDummy[/b]。可选子模组 [b]SuQingTrainingDummyViewpoint[/b] 增加同款三维模型，需要主模组及 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Project Viewpoint[/url]。本体随附四方向普通贴图。使用 Viewpoint 0.1.7 时，在主菜单的模组列表启用主模组和配套模型，再到 Viewpoint 的图形设置 → 资源包（Graphics → Resource Packs）启用训练假人资源包。Viewpoint 0.1.6.2 用户可直接在存档启用配套模型。停用主模组前请先打坏已放置的假人。

[h1]SuQing's Training Dummy[/h1]
Build a training dummy at your base and practice axe, long blunt, short blunt, long blade, short blade and spear weapons with normal attacks. Valid hits award XP for the weapon's vanilla skill.

[list]
[*]Find the dummy under SuQing's Training Dummy in the vanilla building menu. Requires Carpentry 1 and a hammer. Uses 4 planks, 6 nails and 4 ripped sheets.
[*]Right-click to inspect durability. The dummy has 300 HP and loses 1 HP per valid melee hit. Repair with a hammer, 1 plank and 1 ripped sheet to restore up to 100 HP.
[*]Melee hits award 0.5 base XP by default. The sandbox range is 0–100, with normal XP multipliers applied.
[*]Experimental firearms training is off by default. An unobstructed aim ray on the same floor from at least 3 tiles away can award 1 base Aiming XP per valid shot by default. Firearm mods and Viewpoint aiming await in-game verification.
[/list]

[b]Build 42.22.2 single-player[/b]. Enable [b]SuQingTrainingDummy[/b] for normal isometric sprites. The optional [b]SuQingTrainingDummyViewpoint[/b] submod adds the matching 3D model and requires the main mod and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Project Viewpoint[/url]. With Viewpoint 0.1.7, enable both mods from the main menu, then enable the Training Dummy resource pack under Graphics → Resource Packs in Viewpoint. With 0.1.6.2, enable the optional model in the save. Break placed dummies before removing the main mod.
